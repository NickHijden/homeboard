Permanent deletion

Delete your account?

This permanently removes your login, account details, private cloud planner and invitations involving your account. Households where you are the sole owner and member, and their planners, are also deleted.

    Shared households you leave keep their planner entries, including entries you added. This device’s planner and saved login will be cleared. Downloaded files and copies on other devices are not erased; clear those separately. Provider backups and security logs follow the provider’s retention settings.

    Export any data you want to keep before continuing. Deletion cannot be undone.
