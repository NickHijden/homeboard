Support preview

Help should be easy to find.

This is a draft support page for the Homeboard beta. A monitored support address will be added before public signup opens.

This page does not currently collect support requests. The development board is available for testing, but it is not a public customer service channel.

Common questions

How do I use Homeboard on a tablet?
Open the board in Safari or Chrome, sign in, and add it to the home screen once the final onboarding flow is available.

What if I cannot see a new item?
Check the saved status, refresh the board, and confirm both devices are using the same household account.

How do I export my information?
The current board includes a backup download and restore flow. The customer help article will explain the final process.

How do I leave or delete a household?
Those account and household controls are part of the customer-ready onboarding phase and will be documented before signup.
