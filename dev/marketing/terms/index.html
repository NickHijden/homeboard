Beta terms outline

This is a planning outline, not approved terms. No public signup or payment is active on this preview.

Proposed beta

The current plan is a 60-day free beta per household, beginning when the household is activated. A partner invitation would belong to the same household and would not create a second trial.

Payment

No payment card or automatic charge is planned during the beta. Any future paid offer, price, tax presentation, and renewal wording require approval before publication.

After beta expiry

The proposed plan includes a 30-day read-only/export grace period. The final access, retention, and deletion rules must be implemented and approved before beta signup.

Still required

  • Define household activation and the authoritative beta end date.
  • Implement server-controlled trial and read-only states.
  • Publish approved business, contact, privacy, and support information.
  • Explain account deletion, leaving a household, and shared-data handling.
