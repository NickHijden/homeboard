Privacy information

This is not final legal text and is not yet suitable for public signup. It is included so we can review the information the final page must cover.

What Homeboard is intended to store

A household account may contain tasks, events, groceries, assignments, completion history, preferences, and account information needed to provide the service.

What this preview stores

This staging preview uses fictional demo content only. It has no public signup form and does not collect marketing leads from this page.

What the final notice must explain

  • Which service providers process account and household data.
  • How long household data and backups are retained.
  • How a person can export, correct, or delete information.
  • What happens when a person leaves a shared household.
  • How optional marketing consent is separated from service messages.

Contact

A monitored privacy/support contact will be published before public beta signup opens.
