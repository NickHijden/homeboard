ONE-TAP ACCESS

Add Homeboard

  1. Tap Safari’s Share button — the square with the arrow.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Homeboard will then appear as an icon.

If you do not see that option, this iPad may be too old for web-app installation. You can still keep Homeboard open in Safari.
